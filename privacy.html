Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Qren Downloader respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use our app.

1. Information We Handle

When you use the app, you may provide TikTok video links. These links are used to provide the app's requested features.

2. Clipboard

The app may access your clipboard only when you choose to paste a link. The app does not intentionally read clipboard contents in the background.

3. Data Collection

We do not intentionally collect personal information through the app unless clearly explained when a feature requests it.

4. Third-Party Services

The app may interact with third-party services such as TikTok. Their handling of your information is governed by their own privacy policies.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the app. However, no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Qren Downloader is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the Qren-legal GitHub repository.
